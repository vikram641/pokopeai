Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · This App uses YouTube API Services.

This policy explains how pokopeai pipeline (the "App"), operated by pokopeai, handles information. The App is a private publishing tool used only by the pokopeai team to upload our own videos to our own social media accounts.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through YouTube API Services.

What we access

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions and tags, and manages playlists on our own YouTube channel.
  • The App does not read, collect or store personal data of viewers or any other users.

Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token for our own channel is stored only as an encrypted secret in our private build environment. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We do not collect or store any YouTube user data or viewer data. The only stored item is the OAuth refresh token for our own channel. It is kept only while the App is in use and is deleted from our build environment immediately if access is revoked or the App is retired. Videos uploaded by the App are our own content and can be deleted at any time from YouTube Studio.

To request deletion of any information related to the App, email gomechg@gmail.com; we respond within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.
